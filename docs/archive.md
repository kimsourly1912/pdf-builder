# Archive and retention

Use Archive on candidate intake, resumes, recipient accounts, templates, workflows, workflow runs, submissions, AI analysis history, hiring applications and hiring pipelines. The central Archive screen offers a record overview, grouped restoration and eligible permanent deletion after typed confirmation.

Archiving an intake also archives its CV, and vice versa. Original PDFs and candidate history remain stored. Active CV workflows are paused; archiving a workflow stops new runs while existing runs continue. Accounts lose login access and sessions while their forms remain stored. Templates stop accepting unassigned public links; existing assignments and submissions remain available. Processing AI analyses cannot be archived until they finish; queued/failed jobs are cancelled.

Restoring does not run a workflow, resend an invitation or spend AI credits. Restored workflows stay paused, and interrupted runs require an explicit retry. Account ban state is restored without clearing a later independent ban.

Workflows, Hiring (both board and list), Incoming Candidates, Resumes, Forms and Hiring pipelines also offer **Delete permanently** beside their archive action. Opening deletion checks eligibility without changing data and names every affected record. Type `DELETE` to confirm. The server verifies the reviewed snapshot, archives eligible active records and deletes them in one database batch; a conflict rolls back the entire operation. Linked intake/CV deletion removes both records and the PDF, while application deletion retains the candidate and CV. Archive remains a separate, reversible choice.

Permanent deletion checks the current revision and related records atomically. Completed submissions are immutable. Accounts with candidate/form links, templates with assignment/submission history, workflows with runs, and runs with account/delivery history must retain that evidence. Eligible deletion queues stored-file cleanup in the same transaction and retains the audit log.

Hiring applications can be permanently removed only before recorded progress, preparation, forms, interviews, offers or outcomes. Their initial receipt event and retry receipts are removed with the unused application; subsequent history remains protected. Application archive hides only that application, preserves its previous hold, stops active application runs and cancels pending events. Restoration restores the previous hold and never replays those events; interrupted runs require an explicit retry. Hiring pipelines can be deleted only when they are not the default inbox and no jobs, applications or workflow definitions/versions reference their published versions. Their unused versions are deleted together. Pipeline archives, including previous archives, appear in the central Archive screen and restoration keeps the pipeline flag synchronized.

The Archive list groups linked CV and intake records into one expandable row. Can delete and Protected history badges show eligibility and concrete reasons before opening deletion confirmation. Deletion still rechecks current related records atomically. Simple archives offer a 12-second Undo archive action using the exact archive revision. Account access changes and interrupted workflows retain their warnings and require restoration through Archive.

Interview processes protect their original CV, approval snapshot, account, preparation run and accepted document links. Archive remains available; eligible permanent deletion explains interview-history blockers and rechecks foreign keys atomically. See [Interview Progress](interview-progress.md).
